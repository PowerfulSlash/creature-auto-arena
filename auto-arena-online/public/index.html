How to play

Buy creatures from the shop, drag them from the bench onto your half of the board (bottom 3 rows). Your level = max units on the board.
Three copies of the same creature merge into a stronger star. Different species sharing a type activate synergies.
Drag items (left of the board) onto a creature. Drag a creature onto the shop panel (or hover + E / right-click) to sell.
D reroll · F buy XP · E sell hovered · Space start fight early · Q lock shop
Interest: +1 gold per 10 gold held (max +5). Win/lose streaks give bonus gold. Outlast the 7 rival trainers.
